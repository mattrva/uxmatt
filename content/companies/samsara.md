Samsara's products are used in cabs, yards and job sites, where a confusing screen costs time or safety. I've spent my career on complex B2B problems like that, and I build research-first design teams, which fits a company that builds with its customers.

**Days 1–30: listen.** Ride along with drivers and walk sites with field teams, and join customer calls with fleet and safety leaders. Meet each design manager one-on-one to understand their teams, goals and blockers.

**Days 31–60: frame.** With Product and Engineering leaders, agree on the few customer problems that matter most across safety, telematics, equipment and workforce. Map where design is strongest and where it's stretched.

**Days 61–90: systemize.** Tune the rituals and tools that keep a distributed team healthy, like critique, research sharing and design system contribution. Set a growth plan with each manager.
